less screen.
more life.

null blocks the apps that take your time. ending a block early means earning it: type a pledge, solve a problem, get a friend's code, or tap a chip you left in another room.

sessions and schedules

block now for a set time, or every night, morning or workday. schedules run even when null is closed.

a real way out

five ways to end early, from a typed pledge to no way out at all. an emergency exit is always there, and always logged.

private by design

no account, no tracking, no servers. apple's screen time runs on your iphone, and so does everything null stores.
